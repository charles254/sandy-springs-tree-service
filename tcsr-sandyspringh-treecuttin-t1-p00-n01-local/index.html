Critical Warning Signs That Demand Immediate Professional Evaluation

Recognizing structural instability in mature trees early prevents devastating structural property damage and costly emergency interventions throughout Roswell neighborhoods. Warning indicators include trunk cavities, root crown fungal mushrooms, dead crown dieback, and soil fissures along root flares.

  • Visible fungal conks or bracket mushrooms fruiting around the root flare
  • Deep vertical trunk fissures and weeping cracks in bark crevices
  • Sudden uncharacteristic trunk lean accompanied by soil heaving at the root zone
  • Co-dominant stems with included bark along tight V-shaped crotches

Do I need a city permit to remove a tree from my property in Roswell?

Under City of Roswell Chapter 21 Tree Preservation Code, removing certain specimen trees or trees in designated buffers requires an approved municipal permit. Certified arborists assist homeowners with tree condition documentation and permit applications.

How do you safeguard lawns and landscaping during tree removal?
